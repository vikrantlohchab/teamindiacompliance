Who needs factory license?

Under the Factories Act, 1948 a premises is a factory if it employs 10 or more workers with power, or 20 or more without power. Operating without a valid licence risks prosecution and closure orders.

The Factories Act is being subsumed into the Occupational Safety, Health and Working Conditions Code, 2020 as rules are notified, so timely, well-documented compliance today makes the transition smoother.

Licensing sits with the state factories or industrial safety department, and processes differ between Haryana, Delhi and Uttar Pradesh.

Frequently asked questions

How many workers make a premises a factory?
10 or more workers where power is used, or 20 or more where it is not, under the Factories Act, 1948.
Do I need plan approval before building?
Yes. Most states require approval of the factory plan before construction or a material expansion.
Do you also handle pollution and fire NOCs?
We coordinate them alongside the factory licence, since the licence often depends on them.
