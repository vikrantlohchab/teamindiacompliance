Compliance support for Delhi employers

Delhi combines a dense services economy with industrial clusters, and employers here deal with central authorities (EPFO, ESIC) and the Delhi labour department at once. We manage both from our Gurugram head office, with on-site support where needed.

Delhi does not levy Professional Tax, so payroll is simpler than in many states — but Delhi Shops & Establishments, minimum wage revisions and Labour Welfare Fund rules still need careful tracking.

Local legal snapshot

  • Shops & Establishments: Delhi Shops & Establishments Act, 1954
  • Professional Tax: not levied in Delhi
  • Minimum wages: notified by the Delhi government and revised periodically
  • Central registrations: EPF and ESIC apply as per headcount thresholds

Frequently asked questions

Which Act covers offices in Delhi?
The Delhi Shops & Establishments Act, 1954 covers commercial establishments and offices.
Is Professional Tax applicable in Delhi?
No. Delhi does not levy Professional Tax.
Can you serve us if we are in Delhi but you are in Gurugram?
Yes. We handle registrations and filings online and attend inspections or hearings in Delhi as required.
