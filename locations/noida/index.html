Compliance support for Noida employers

Noida and Greater Noida host IT parks, electronics and industrial units, and a large contract workforce. State law here is Uttar Pradesh law, which differs from Delhi and Haryana, even though the offices sit in the same region.

We handle registrations, monthly filings and vendor compliance for Noida units from our Gurugram head office, with on-site support as needed.

Local legal snapshot

  • Shops & Establishments: Uttar Pradesh Dookan Aur Vanijya Adhishthan Adhiniyam, 1962
  • Professional Tax: not levied in Uttar Pradesh
  • Contract labour: CLRA registration and licences apply above the threshold
  • Central registrations: EPF and ESIC apply as per headcount thresholds

Frequently asked questions

Does Noida follow Delhi's labour rules?
No. Noida is in Uttar Pradesh, so UP state laws apply, including its own Shops & Establishments Act.
Do you handle contractor compliance for Noida factories?
Yes. We manage CLRA registration, contractor licences and monthly vendor compliance checks.
Can you register an office in Noida remotely?
Yes. Most registrations are online and we coordinate offline steps with the local authorities.
