Compliance support in Bawal

Bawal, in Rewari district, is one of Haryana's industrial areas and sits on the Delhi–Jaipur corridor. Plants here typically run large shop floors with contract manpower, so industrial compliance has to be systematic.

We help Bawal units set up registrations and run monthly compliance, and we stand behind you in inspections.

What Bawal employers typically need

  • Factory licence and plan approvals for new or expanding plants
  • Contract-labour licences and contractor compliance checks
  • EPF and ESIC for permanent and contract workers
  • Statutory registers and monthly filings
  • Government liaisoning for notices and inspections

Local legal snapshot

  • State: Haryana. Shops and offices fall under the Haryana Shops and Commercial Establishments Act, 1958.
  • Factory licensing: Haryana Department of Industrial Safety and Health.
  • Professional Tax: not levied in Haryana.
  • Central registrations: EPF and ESIC apply as per headcount thresholds. The PF wage ceiling is ₹25,000 a month from 17 September 2026 (summary).
